Class FilterRowRules

What the filter row hides from the catalog, each part with its reasons: the drawing default's rules (CatalogVisibilityRules), then Show's family rule, then the deck height. The same answer the catalog panel lists and PBSTAMP reads when it says a part it is placing is hidden.

Apply(IEnumerable<LibraryEntry>, FilterRow)

Splits a catalog pool by the filter row. IndependentItems keeps only the independent families and judges them by the region alone (they stand on their own, so no structure's age, system or IPEMA flag reads them, and no deck holds them); the parts outside that pool are neither shown nor hidden. StructureParts judges the pool as a play structure (the independent families are hidden, "not a structure part"); All applies no family rule. Both then narrow by the deck height (DeckHeightReason(LibraryEntry, double, StructureSettings)), its reason added after the settings' own.

DeckHeightReason(LibraryEntry, double, StructureSettings)

Why a part does not suit a deck of the row's height, or null when it does. A deck must be rated at that height by the row's settings (CheckFor(double, IReadOnlyList<AgeFit>, StructureSettings): its age band, and the 108" cap where the row caps), said "not rated for 48""; a part that attaches to a deck must fit a deck of that height by its age envelopes (FitsHostHeight(IReadOnlyList<AgeFit>, double?, string?, string?), the rule PBFIT's height check reads), said "not for a 48" deck". Any other part, and a part with no age data, is not judged by a deck height: missing data never hides a part.

KeptOnDefaultChange(StructureSettings?, StructureSettings, StructureSettings, string?)

What a built structure keeps when the drawing default changes under it (the user's ruling, 2026-10-20: switching the row to start the next structure leaves the built ones as they were). Every setting that is changing, and that the structure was following (no value of its own), is saved onto it at the old default's value; settings that are not changing stay inherited. A structure that is not a play structure has its system, mounting and material forced, so only its age and IPEMA flag are saved. An old value that was not set cannot be saved (the record has no "not set, of its own"), and an IPEMA flag can only be saved on: a structure cannot opt out of an IPEMA-only default.
