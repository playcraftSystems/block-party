Class RoofHeightRule

The roof height rule (execution plan Item 20): a roof, shade canopy or topper stands at the highest deck under its posts, rounded up to whole feet, plus the part's own minimum height for the structure's age range, plus the drawing's roof height override (roofs and canopies only). With no deck under it, it stands at the minimum alone. A designer may raise it in 12" steps, up to the part's maximum; the raise is never stored, because it is read back from the height and the rule whenever a deck changes.

Check(double, RoofHeight, string?)

Judges a placed part's height against its rule: at the rule is right; above it by whole 12" steps, up to the maximum, is a deliberate raise; below it, off the 12" steps, or above the maximum is a finding. A roof or canopy above the ground height with no deck under it is a finding too (a topper on a post with no deck may be raised).

Follow(double, RoofHeight, RoofHeight)

The height a part goes to when the decks under it change: its new rule plus the raise it had over its old rule, when that raise was whole 12" steps and the result stays at or below the maximum. Otherwise its new rule, with a note saying why the raise went; and never above the maximum, which is where it stops (with a note) when even its new rule is higher.
