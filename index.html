Playcraft Suite#

Design the playground in the drawing. Playcraft Suite turns AutoCAD 2027 into a playground design studio. Components place with real catalog geometry and snap to their actual mate points, support posts generate and count themselves, and the drawing produces its own priced bill of materials from the live Playcraft catalog.

Post counts, shared-corner math, part lookups, price sheets: everything designers track by hand today is derived from the drawing instead, every time, so it is never stale and never duplicated. Move a deck and re-run. The posts, the counts, and the dollars follow.

  • Place decks and components with catalog-true geometry that snaps flush and mates correctly (PBCATALOG, PBATTACH)
  • Generate support posts from real slot data, with shared corners merged exactly as the engineering rules require (PBGEN)
  • Count and price with an instant bill of materials that works offline and fetches live pricing on demand (PBBOM, PBPRICE, PBSYNC)
  • Round-trip legacy design files (.kcd): import them with exact placement, extend them with new components, and write them back for the rest of the toolchain, plus a modern JSON copy for web and API use (PBKCDINFO, PBIMPORTKCD, PBEXPORTKCD)
